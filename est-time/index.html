Eastern Time: EST and EDT

Eastern Standard Time (EST) is UTC−5. From 2 a.m. on the second Sunday in March to 2 a.m. on the first Sunday in November, the Eastern zone uses Eastern Daylight Time (EDT), UTC−4. In 2026 the zone returns to EST on Sunday, November 1. New York, Washington, Atlanta, Miami and Detroit are on Eastern Time.

2027Sunday, March 14Sunday, November 7
2028Sunday, March 12Sunday, November 5

The dates come from federal law (15 U.S.C. 260a). People often say “EST” all year; strictly, summer time is EDT. “ET” covers both.

Convert Eastern Time

On Monday, October 19, 9:00 a.m. Eastern Time is 6:00 a.m. Pacific Time.

London is usually 5 hours ahead. Because the UK and US change their clocks on different Sundays, the gap is 4 hours for about three weeks in March and one week around the end of October.

Where Eastern Time applies

The Eastern zone covers the eastern United States west of 67°30′ W and includes all of Maine (49 CFR 71.4). Its western edge is the line between the Eastern and Central zones, which crosses Michigan, Indiana, Kentucky, Tennessee and Florida, so parts of those states use Central Time (49 CFR 71.5).

On a night shift

When standard time returns on November 1, 2026, the hour from 1:00 to 2:00 a.m. Eastern happens twice, so a 12-hour night shift lasts 13 hours; in March it lasts 11. The night shift clock change calculator works out the hours and pay. For pay periods and holidays, see the 2027 pay period calendar.

Questions

What time zone is EST in the USA?

Eastern Standard Time, UTC−5, used in the eastern United States in winter. In summer the same zone uses EDT, UTC−4.

Is New York on EST or EDT right now?

New York uses EDT from the second Sunday in March to the first Sunday in November and EST the rest of the year. In 2026 it switches to EST on Sunday, November 1.

What is the difference between EST and ET?

EST is the winter standard time (UTC−5). ET, Eastern Time, means whichever is in use: EST in winter, EDT in summer.

How many hours ahead is EST from PST?

3 hours. Noon Eastern is 9:00 a.m. Pacific, all year.

How far ahead of EST is London?

Usually 5 hours. For about three weeks in March and one week at the end of October it is 4, because the UK and US change clocks on different dates.
