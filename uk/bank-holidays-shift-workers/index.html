Working on a bank holiday

There is no legal right to have bank holidays off or to be paid more for working them. Employers can count them towards the 5.6 weeks of paid holiday (Working Time Regulations, regs. 13 and 13A). Many contracts pay time and a half or double time, or give a day off in lieu: check yours.

Dates are worked out from the rules for each holiday and the substitute-day rule. The government can add a one-off bank holiday by proclamation, so check gov.uk before you plan around a date.

Questions

What happens when a bank holiday falls on a weekend?

A substitute day is given, usually the next Monday. When Christmas Day and Boxing Day both fall on a weekend, the substitute days are the following Monday and Tuesday.

Does Scotland have different bank holidays?

Yes: 2 January and St Andrew's Day, the summer bank holiday on the first Monday of August and no Easter Monday.
