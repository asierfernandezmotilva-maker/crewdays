Rotas

  • 4 on 4 off Rota16-day cycle, 42 h a weekA 4 on 4 off rota is four 12-hour shifts followed by four days off, an 8-day cycle, so your working days move through the week.
  • Continental Shift Pattern8-day cycle, 42 h a weekThe continental shift pattern is 2 days, 2 nights and 4 days off on 12-hour shifts, an 8-day cycle for four teams.
  • 2-2-3 Rota (Pitman)14-day cycle, 42 h a weekThe 2-2-3 rota, also called the Pitman, repeats every 14 days: 2 on, 2 off, 3 on, 2 off, 2 on, 3 off on 12-hour shifts.
  • 3 on 3 off Rota12-day cycle, 42 h a weekA 3 on 3 off rota is three 12-hour shifts and three days off.
  • 2 on 2 off Rota8-day cycle, 42 h a weekA 2 on 2 off rota is two 12-hour shifts and two days off.
  • DuPont Rota28-day cycle, 42 h a weekThe DuPont rota is a 28-day pattern of 12-hour shifts for four teams: 4 nights, 3 off, 3 days, 1 off, 3 nights, 3 off, 4 days, then 7 days off.
  • Earlies, Lates and Nights Rota28-day cycle, 42 h a weekAn earlies, lates and nights rota rotates 8-hour shifts: here 7 earlies, 2 off, 7 lates, 2 off, 7 nights, 3 off, a 28-day cycle for four teams.

Questions

Does it show UK bank holidays?

Yes: England and Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland. Choose under "Holidays shown". Substitute days are included when a bank holiday falls on a weekend.

Do I get paid extra for working a bank holiday?

Only if your contract says so. UK law gives no right to time off or extra pay on bank holidays; they can count towards your 5.6 weeks of holiday.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything stays in your browser.
