What is a Hitch Rotation schedule?

A hitch rotation (FIFO roster in Australia, fly in, fly out) is a block of 12-hour shifts on site followed by a block at home. The most common are 14 days on and 14 off (even time), 7 on and 7 off, 14 on and 7 off (often called 2 and 1), 21 on and 7 off, 28 on and 28 off and 8 on and 6 off. Offshore platforms, oilfields, mines and remote camps use them, and 7 on 7 off is also common in hospitals.

Working a Hitch Rotation schedule

On a hitch rotation the crew change date is what matters: count from it and every later hitch follows. Put your first day on site as the start date in the calendar and choose your rotation; the calendar then shows each hitch and each day at home for the whole year.

Travel days are handled differently by each employer: some count them as working days, some as days off and some pay them separately. If your travel day is part of the hitch, include it when you set the start date.

Even-time rotations (7/7, 14/14, 28/28) need two crews for each position on day shift, and four for day and night. A 14/7 needs three crews and a 21/7 four, because the time on site is longer than the time at home.

Overtime on a Hitch Rotation schedule: it depends on the workweek

Federal overtime is due for hours over 40 in a workweek, a fixed 7-day period that your employer chooses and that does not have to start on Monday (29 CFR 778.105). With the same shifts, the day it starts changes how the hours fall into weeks, and so the overtime. Crew A, cycle starting on a Monday:

Hours a year = hours worked in one cycle × 365 ÷ days in the cycle. 14 on 14 off: 168 h × 365 ÷ 28 = 2,190 h. These are scheduled hours; overtime, leave and swaps change your own total.

Pros of the Hitch Rotation schedule

  • Long, predictable breaks at home.
  • Easy to plan holidays and travel around.
  • Even time (7/7, 14/14, 28/28) gives half the year at home.

Cons

  • Long stretches away from family.
  • Travel days are often unpaid or count as days off.
  • Rotations differ from site to site, so check your own crew change date.

Questions

How many hours a week is a Hitch Rotation schedule?

On average 42 hours a week: 14 shifts of 12 hours every 28 days. Over a year that is about 2,190 scheduled hours, before overtime, leave or Kelly days.

How many crews does a Hitch Rotation schedule need?

2 crews for this version, with exactly one crew on each shift every day.

How long is the Hitch Rotation schedule cycle?

28 days. After that the same days repeat, so the calendar for any year follows from one start date.

How do I add my shifts to Google Calendar or my iPhone?

Pick your pattern and a day you start a block, press "Add to calendar" and open the file. Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and Outlook import it as all-day events, so daylight saving time does not move them.

Can my family see my schedule?

Yes. Press "Copy link" ("Share" on a phone) and send it. The link holds your pattern and start date, so it opens the same calendar on any phone. Nobody needs an account.

Can I download it to Excel?

Yes. Press "Excel" to download the year as a CSV file that opens in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers. With "All crews" selected you get one column per crew, ready to print or post for the whole team.
