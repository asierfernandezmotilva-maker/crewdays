Firefighter Shift Calendar (A, B, C Shift)

Most US fire departments run 24-hour shifts with three platoons, usually called A, B and C shift, on a 24/48, 48/96 or Kelly rotation. Departments moving to four platoons use the 24/72.

Who is covered by 7(k)

The 7(k) overtime rules cover fire department employees who are trained in fire suppression, have the legal authority and responsibility to fight fires, and respond to fires and emergencies. That includes paramedics, EMTs and rescue workers employed by the fire department who meet those conditions (29 CFR 553.210). Civilian dispatchers, mechanics and clerks are not covered and get overtime after 40 hours a week.

Trading shifts

Two firefighters can swap shifts voluntarily, with the department's approval. The hours worked as a substitute are left out of the overtime count, and each person is credited as if they had worked their normal schedule (29 CFR 553.31). The swap must be freely chosen: the department can suggest it but cannot penalise anyone who refuses.

Holidays on a three-platoon schedule

On a 24/48 each platoon works one day in three, so every holiday is worked by one platoon. Because a normal year has 365 days, the platoon that works a fixed-date holiday such as July 4 changes from one year to the next. Choose "All crews" and change the year to see who works each holiday.
