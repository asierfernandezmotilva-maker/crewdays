Time and a half calculator

Time and a half is 1.5 times your regular hourly rate, so $20 an hour becomes $30 and $25 becomes $37.50. Federal law requires it for every hour over 40 in a workweek. On 12-hour shifts, a 48-hour week at $25 pays $1,300.00: 40 hours at $25 and 8 at $37.50.

Time and a half calculator

Time and a half: $37.50 an hour. 8 overtime hours add $100.00 on top of straight pay. Week: $1,300.00 before tax.

Federal rule: time and a half after 40 hours in the workweek, on a regular rate that includes the other pay. California: hours over 8 in a day also count, and hours over 12 in a day are double time (this calculator pays them at 1.5). Police and fire departments on a 7(k) work period use the 7(k) overtime calculator.

How to calculate time and a half

  1. Multiply your hourly rate by 1.5. That is your time and a half rate: $20 × 1.5 = $30.
  2. Count the hours over 40 in the workweek. Those are your overtime hours.
  3. Multiply the overtime hours by the time and a half rate and add your pay for the first 40 hours.

Example: 48 hours at $25. Regular pay is 40 × $25 = $1,000. Overtime is 8 × $37.50 = $300. The week pays $1,300.

The law says “one and one-half times the regular rate”, not the base rate (29 U.S.C. 207(a)(1)). If you earn a night differential or a bonus in the week, it goes into the regular rate first (29 CFR 778.109). The shift differential guide works one week through step by step.

Time and a half on 12-hour shifts

Is a 12-hour shift overtime? Not under federal law: only hours over 40 in the workweek count, however long each shift is. With 12-hour shifts the 40-hour line falls in the middle of a shift. Three shifts a week is 36 hours and no overtime. Four is 48 hours, 8 of them overtime. Rotations that switch between 3 and 4 shifts a week land on both sides, so the overtime depends on the day your workweek starts.

A Pitman crew works about 2,190 hours a year on average whatever the workweek, but its overtime runs from 208 to 520 hours: the employer chooses the start day once and must keep it (29 CFR 778.105). A 24/48 schedule averages 56 hours a week, so it carries the most overtime under the 40-hour rule; fire departments usually pay it under the 7(k) exemption, with a higher threshold. Is a 12-hour shift overtime in California?

California also pays time and a half for hours over 8 in a workday and for the first 8 hours on the seventh day in a row in a workweek. Hours over 12 in a day are double time (California Labor Code 510). On three 12-hour shifts, 4 hours of each shift are daily overtime: 12 hours a week, $150.00 of premium at $25, unless the employer has a valid alternative workweek schedule. Other states with daily rules are listed in overtime after 8 or 12 hours.

When time and a half is required

  • Required: hours over 40 in the workweek for nonexempt employees (29 U.S.C. 207(a)(1)). Each workweek stands alone: a short week does not offset a long one (29 CFR 778.104).
  • Not required by federal law: weekends, holidays, nights or a sixth day as such. That extra pay comes from your contract or policy. Holiday rules are in the holiday pay calculator.
  • Different threshold: firefighters and police on a 7(k) work period, for example 212 hours in 28 days for fire protection (29 CFR 553.230).

Taxes on time and a half

Overtime is taxed as wages. For 2025 through 2028 there is a federal deduction for the part of overtime pay “that exceeds their regular rate of pay”, up to $12,500 a year, or $25,000 filing jointly (IRS). It covers only the extra half. At $25 an hour, a Pitman crew's 208 to 520 overtime hours make $2,600 to $6,500 of deductible premium.

Questions

How do you calculate time and a half?

Multiply your hourly rate by 1.5 and pay that rate for each hour over 40 in the workweek. At $20 an hour, time and a half is $30, so 45 hours pays 40 × $20 + 5 × $30 = $950.

What is time and a half for $20 an hour?

$30 an hour. Eight hours of overtime at that rate is $240, and a 12-hour overtime shift is $360.

Does time and a half start after 8 hours or 40 hours?

Under federal law, after 40 hours in the workweek. California, Alaska and Nevada (for lower-paid workers) also count hours over 8 in a day, and Colorado over 12.

Is time and a half required on weekends and holidays?

Not by federal law. The FLSA requires it only for hours over 40 in the week. Weekend or holiday premiums come from your employer's policy or a union contract.

Is time and a half based on my base rate?

On your regular rate, which includes night differentials, shift premiums and most bonuses earned in the week, divided by the hours worked (29 CFR 778.109).
