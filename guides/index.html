Guides for shift workers

Hours, pay and schedules explained with real numbers. Each guide is written by Asier Fernández and checked against the official rules it quotes.

  • How many work hours are in a year?2,080 is the usual answer, but 2027 has 261 weekdays: 2,088 work hours. Every year and month, plus the real yearly hours of 12- and 24-hour shifts.
  • 1st, 2nd and 3rd shift hours1st shift usually runs 7 a.m. to 3 p.m., 2nd 3 p.m. to 11 p.m. and 3rd (graveyard) 11 p.m. to 7 a.m. Common 8, 10 and 12-hour times and how night pay works.
  • Swing shift: hours and schedulesSwing shift is the evening shift (often 3 p.m. to 11 p.m.) or a schedule that swings between days and nights. Hours, pay and rotating swing schedules.

  • Military time chartMilitary time chart from 0000 to 2330 with the regular 12-hour time, plus a converter both ways. How to read and say military time, midnight and noon.
  • Hourly paycheck calculatorEstimate 2026 take-home pay from an hourly wage: overtime, shift differential, Social Security, Medicare and federal tax with the 2026 IRS brackets.
  • Salary calculator: hourly to yearlyTurn an hourly wage into yearly, monthly, biweekly and weekly pay for a 40-hour week or a 36, 42 or 56-hour shift schedule, with a $15 to $50 table.
  • Federal income tax calculator 2026Estimate 2026 federal income tax with the new IRS brackets and standard deduction, plus the overtime deduction, for single or married filing jointly.
  • Daylight saving time: when the clocks changeDaylight saving time ends Sunday, November 1, 2026 at 2 a.m. in the US and October 25 in the UK. Every date to 2030, the law and night shifts.
  • Sleep calculatorFind the best time to go to bed or wake up in 90-minute sleep cycles, with a night shift mode for sleeping during the day. Based on CDC and NIOSH guidance.
  • How many weeks are in a year?A year has 52 weeks and one day, or two in a leap year. Why 2026 has 53 numbered ISO weeks, the week number of any date and what it means for paychecks.
  • Date calculatorCount the days between two dates (with weekdays) or add and subtract days from a date. Useful for notice periods, probation, leave and shift cycles.
  • Minimum wage by state (2026)The 2026 minimum wage in every state and DC from the US Department of Labor, the federal $7.25, daily overtime rules and a weekly and yearly pay calculator.
  • Baylor shift: the weekend nursing scheduleA Baylor shift is weekend-only nursing work, usually two 12-hour shifts on Saturday and Sunday at a premium. Hours a year, overtime and what to ask.
  • Clopening: closing and opening the next dayA clopening is closing at night and opening next morning. No federal law bans it; Oregon requires 10 hours of rest or time and a half. Your rights.
  • 5-5-4 scheduleThe 5-5-4 schedule: 5 days, 5 off, 4 nights, 1 off, a 15-day cycle of 12-hour shifts for three crews. 50.4 hours a week on average. Calendar and math.
  • 3-4-4-3 scheduleThe 3-4-4-3 schedule: 3 on, 4 off, 4 on, 3 off, a 14-day cycle of 12-hour shifts at 42 hours a week. Weekends, overtime and how it differs from the 4-3-3-4.
  • 12-hour rotating shift schedule with 3 teamsHow to cover 24/7 with three teams on 12-hour shifts: the 2 days, 2 nights, 2 off rotation, the hours it means (56 a week) and the overtime to budget for.
  • How many days in a row can you legally work?No federal limit on days in a row for adults. California bars more than six days in seven; Maine caps mandatory overtime. Rules for minors and drivers.
  • On-call pay: when on-call time is workWhen on-call time counts as paid work under federal law: on the premises or too restricted to use the time. Examples, night calls and 24-hour shifts.
  • Mandatory overtime laws for nursesWhich states limit mandatory overtime for nurses, with each statute: Washington, Minnesota, West Virginia and Maine. Exceptions and federal rules.
  • PTO on 12-hour shiftsPTO is counted in hours, so each 12-hour shift off uses 12. How many shifts 80, 120 or 160 hours give, how accrual works and how to stretch your leave.
  • 24-hour shift schedule templateFree 24-hour shift templates for fire and EMS: 24/48, 48/96, Kelly, 24/72 and 1-3-2-3, with hours and a printable year for every platoon.
  • How many night shifts in a row can you work in the UK?UK law sets no maximum number of night shifts in a row. It limits night workers to an average of 8 hours in 24 and requires daily and weekly rest.
  • Southern swing scheduleThe southern swing rotates four crews through days, evenings and nights on 8-hour shifts: 7 on, 2 off, 7 on, 2 off, 7 on, 3 off. Hours, weekends, calendar.
  • 8-hour shift schedule with every other weekend offAn 8-hour, 40-hour-a-week schedule with every other weekend off: the two-week pattern, how two people cover all seven days and the overtime to watch.
  • 1-3-2-3 firefighter scheduleOne 24-hour shift, 3 days off, two 24s, 3 off: the 1-3-2-3 firefighter schedule. 56 hours a week with three platoons. Calendar and 7(k) overtime.
  • 5-2-5-3 work scheduleThe 5-2-5-3 schedule is 5 on, 2 off, 5 on, 3 off: a 15-day cycle of 8-hour shifts for three squads. Hours a week, weekends off and daily coverage.
  • 4-5-5 shift pattern (4/5/5/4/5/5)The 4-5-5 shift pattern is 4 on, 5 off, 5 on, 4 off, 5 on, 5 off: a 28-day cycle of 12-hour shifts. Two crews cover every day; 42 hours a week.
  • UTC time nowCurrent UTC time, what Coordinated Universal Time is and how far each US time zone is behind it: EST is UTC−5, PST UTC−8, one hour less in summer.
  • Eastern Time: EST and EDTEastern Time is EST (UTC−5) in winter and EDT (UTC−4) from March to November. Current time, the 2026–2028 change dates, conversions and where it applies.
  • Central Time: CST and CDTCentral Time is CST (UTC−6) in winter and CDT (UTC−5) in summer, one hour behind Eastern. Current time, where it applies, Texas and conversions.
  • PST to ESTPacific Time is 3 hours behind Eastern Time all year: 9 a.m. PST is 12 p.m. EST. Hour-by-hour table, a converter and shift handover examples.
