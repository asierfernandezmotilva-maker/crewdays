What is a 2 on 2 off schedule?

A 2 on 2 off schedule is two 12-hour shifts followed by two days off, a 4-day cycle. The rotating version is 2 days, 2 off, 2 nights, 2 off, an 8-day cycle for four crews.

Working a 2 on 2 off schedule

The 2 on 2 off schedule is the simplest 12-hour pattern: two shifts, two days off, a 4-day cycle. You never work more than two long shifts in a row, which many people find easier on the body than blocks of three or four.

The days off are short, though. Two days rarely feel like a real break, especially after nights, and the pattern gives fewer full weekends than the Pitman or Panama, which group the off days into a three-day weekend.

Many employers turn the 2-2 into a 2-2-3 by adding a three-day block every week; that is the Pitman. If your rota looks like 2 on 2 off with an occasional 3, check the Pitman calendar too.

Overtime on a 2 on 2 off schedule: it depends on the workweek

Federal overtime is due for hours over 40 in a workweek, a fixed 7-day period that your employer chooses and that does not have to start on Monday (29 CFR 778.105). With the same shifts, the day it starts changes how the hours fall into weeks, and so the overtime. Crew A, cycle starting on a Monday:

Hours a year = hours worked in one cycle × 365 ÷ days in the cycle. Days only: 24 h × 365 ÷ 4 = 2,190 h. These are scheduled hours; overtime, leave and swaps change your own total.

Pros of the 2 on 2 off schedule

  • Never more than two long shifts in a row.
  • Frequent days off.
  • Easy to cover with two crews.

Cons

  • Breaks are only two days.
  • Weekends off come up irregularly.
  • The rotating version changes between days and nights every few days.

Questions

How many hours a week is a 2 on 2 off schedule?

On average 42 hours a week: 2 shifts of 12 hours every 4 days. Over a year that is about 2,190 scheduled hours, before overtime, leave or Kelly days.

How many crews does a 2 on 2 off schedule need?

2 crews for this version, with exactly one crew on each shift every day.

How long is the 2 on 2 off schedule cycle?

4 days. After that the same days repeat, so the calendar for any year follows from one start date.

How do I add my shifts to Google Calendar or my iPhone?

Pick your pattern and a day you start a block, press "Add to calendar" and open the file. Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and Outlook import it as all-day events, so daylight saving time does not move them.

Can my family see my schedule?

Yes. Press "Copy link" ("Share" on a phone) and send it. The link holds your pattern and start date, so it opens the same calendar on any phone. Nobody needs an account.

Can I download it to Excel?

Yes. Press "Excel" to download the year as a CSV file that opens in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers. With "All crews" selected you get one column per crew, ready to print or post for the whole team.
