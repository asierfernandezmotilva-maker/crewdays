Every 24-hour schedule is a short cycle repeated all year. Pick yours below, copy the cycle or open it in the calendar, and you get a template for every platoon that you can print, download for Excel or add to a phone.

Free template: 24/48 and 24/72 for 2027

This template covers every day of 2027 for the two most common 24-hour rotations: the 24/48 with platoons A, B and C, and the 24/72 with A, B, C and D. Platoon A works Friday, January 1, 2027, and each platoon comes on the day after the one before it, starting at the same hour every shift.

One row per day: date, weekday, the 24/48 platoon on duty and the 24/72 platoon on duty. If your department's platoon A is on duty on another date, swap the letters, or build your own year in the shift calendar.

How to make the template for your department

  1. Choose the schedule and the date platoon A starts a shift in the shift calendar.
  2. Under “Show”, pick “All crews” to see A, B and C (and D on a 24/72) side by side, with your department's colors under “Crew names”.
  3. Press “Print” for a wall calendar, “Excel” for a spreadsheet with one column per platoon, or “Embed” to put “who is on duty today” on the station website.
  4. Add Kelly days under “Kelly days” if your department uses them.

Building a template by hand

Write the cycle once, one letter per day, then shift it for each platoon: on a 24/48 platoon B starts one day after A and platoon C two days after. In Excel, a formula such as =MID("XOO", MOD(date - start, 3) + 1, 1) returns the state of any date for a 3-day cycle. The calendar does the same for every day of the year and every platoon at once, and handles leap years and the clock changes.

Which one to choose

The 24/48 is the most common; the 48/96 halves the commutes; the 24/72 needs a fourth platoon but brings the week down to 42 hours. Compare them in 48/96 vs 24/48 and on firefighter shift schedules.

Questions

How do you make a 24-hour shift schedule?

Write the cycle (for a 24/48: one day on, two off), give each platoon its own start date one day apart and repeat it for the year. The shift calendar does this for every platoon at once.

What is the most common 24-hour shift schedule?

The 24/48: one 24-hour shift, then 48 hours off, with three platoons (A, B and C).

How many hours is a 24-hour shift schedule a week?

56 on average with three platoons (24/48, 48/96, Kelly) and 42 with four (24/72).

Can I download a 24-hour schedule template for Excel?

Yes: open your schedule in the calendar, choose All crews and press Excel to download the year with one column per platoon.

What is a 24-hour shift template with 4 platoons?

The 24/72: one 24-hour shift and 72 hours off, platoons A to D one day apart.

How many days a month do you work on a 24/48?

About 10: between 9 and 11 shifts a month for platoon A in 2027, because a 3-day cycle does not fit the months evenly.

How many shifts a year is a 24/72?

91 or 92 24-hour shifts in 2027, depending on the platoon: 2,184 to 2,208 hours.

Do you get paid for sleeping on a 24-hour shift?

Firefighters and police under section 7(k): yes on a 24-hour shift, because sleep time can only be excluded from tours of more than 24 hours (29 CFR 553.222). Other employees on duty for 24 hours or more may agree to exclude a regular sleep period of up to 8 hours if there are adequate sleeping facilities; calls that interrupt sleep are paid, and if you cannot get at least 5 hours of sleep the whole period is paid (29 CFR 785.22).
