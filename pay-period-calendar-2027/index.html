Is 2027 a 27-paycheck year?

A year has 52 weeks and one day (two in a leap year), so the paydays slowly move earlier. Every 11 years or so a biweekly payroll fits 27 paydays into one year instead of 26, and a weekly payroll fits 53 instead of 52. 2027 starts on a Friday and is not a leap year, so it has 53 Fridays.

  • Paid every Friday: 53 paydays in 2027.
  • Paid every other Friday: 27 paydays only if the first one is Friday, January 1, 2027. That day is New Year's Day, so many employers pay on Thursday, December 31, 2026 instead, and 2027 then has 26 (26 if your first payday is January 8, 27 if it is January 15).

Federal pay periods 2027

Federal employees are paid every two weeks on a Sunday-to-Saturday cycle. The leave year begins with the first day of the first complete pay period of the calendar year (5 CFR 630.201), so the 2027 leave year runs from Sunday, January 10, 2027 to Saturday, January 8, 2028: 26 pay periods.

Payday comes a few days after each period ends and depends on your payroll provider (NFC, DFAS, DOI IBC or GSA), so check your agency's calendar for the exact date. Annual leave above the carryover limit (usually 240 hours, 5 U.S.C. 6304) is forfeited at the end of the leave year, so the last day to use 2027 leave is Saturday, January 8, 2028.

What a 27th paycheck means

If you are paid by the hour, a 27th payday is simply one more paycheck for two more weeks of work. If you are on a yearly salary, check how your employer divides it: some divide the salary by 27 that year, so each paycheck is a little smaller; others keep 26 equal paychecks and pay the extra one on top. Deductions taken per paycheck, such as health premiums or retirement contributions, may also come out 27 times.

Shift workers paid every two weeks can see their paydays next to their shifts: enter “A payday” under “Your pay” in the shift calendar. The shift pay calculator works out yearly pay by pattern.

Questions

How many pay periods are in 2027?

26 for most biweekly payrolls and 52 for weekly ones. You get 27 biweekly paydays only if the first is Friday, January 1, 2027, and 53 weekly paydays if you are paid on Fridays.

How many Fridays are in 2027?

53. 2027 starts and ends on a Friday.

Which months have three paychecks in 2027?

With Friday paydays starting January 8: April and October. Starting January 15: January and July and December.

When does the 2027 federal leave year start?

On Sunday, January 10, 2027, the first day of the first complete pay period of 2027 (5 CFR 630.201). It ends on Saturday, January 8, 2028, after 26 pay periods.

Is 2026 a 27-paycheck year?

On the calendar, only for biweekly payrolls whose first payday of 2026 was Thursday, January 1. Payrolls paid every other Friday from January 2 can also reach 27 if the employer moves the January 1, 2027 payday (New Year's Day) back to Thursday, December 31, 2026. Weekly payrolls paid on Thursday get 53 paydays in 2026.
